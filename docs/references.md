# References

This page lists the main technical references used in the INOAS project and in
the final Student Aerospace Challenge report. Third-party books, articles and
technical reports are cited here but are not redistributed in this repository.

## Spacecraft Systems and Orbital Dynamics

- P. Fortescue, J. Stark, and G. Swinerd, *Spacecraft Systems Engineering*,
  2011.
- NASA, "State-of-the-Art of Small Spacecraft Technology," National Aeronautics
  and Space Administration, Technical Report, 2024.
- D. A. Vallado, *Fundamentals of Astrodynamics and Applications*, 4th ed.,
  Microcosm Press, 2013.
- W. Fehse, *Automated Rendezvous and Docking of Spacecraft*, 2003.
- J. R. Wertz and W. J. Larson, *Space Mission Analysis and Design*, 3rd ed.,
  Microcosm Press, 1999.
- W. H. Clohessy and R. S. Wiltshire, "Terminal guidance system for satellite
  rendezvous," *Journal of the Aerospace Sciences*, vol. 27, no. 9,
  pp. 653-658, 1960.
- N. K. Pavlis, S. A. Holmes, S. C. Kenyon, and J. K. Factor, "The development
  and evaluation of the Earth Gravitational Model 2008 (EGM2008),"
  *Journal of Geophysical Research: Solid Earth*, vol. 117, no. B4, p. B04406,
  2012.
- D. Brouwer, "Solution of the problem of artificial satellite theory without
  drag," *The Astronomical Journal*, vol. 64, pp. 378-397, 1959.

## GNSS, Navigation, and Estimation

- E. Kaplan and C. Hegarty, *Understanding GPS/GNSS: Principles and
  Applications*, 3rd ed., 2017.
- M. L. Psiaki, "Block acquisition of weak GPS signals in a software receiver,"
  *Proceedings of the ION GPS Conference*, 2001.
- European Space Agency, "GNSS for Space Applications," ESA Technical Report,
  2020.
- N. Linty, L. Lo Presti, F. Dovis, and P. Crosta, "Performance analysis of
  duty-cycle power saving techniques in GNSS mass-market receivers," in
  *Proceedings of the IEEE/ION Position, Location and Navigation Symposium
  (PLANS)*, 2014, pp. 1096-1104.
- V. Bellad, M. G. Petovello, and G. Lachapelle, "Tracking and position errors
  in GNSS receivers with intermittent signal tracking," *Navigation*, vol. 63,
  no. 2, pp. 193-204, 2016.
- J. Sanz Subirana, J. M. Juan Zornoza, and M. Hernandez-Pajares, *GNSS Data
  Processing, Volume I: Fundamentals and Algorithms*. Noordwijk, Netherlands:
  ESA Communications, 2013, ESA TM-23/1.
- L. Tang, J. Wang, B. Cui, H. Zhu, and M. Ge, "Multi-GNSS precise point
  positioning with predicted orbits and clocks," *GPS Solutions*, vol. 27,
  p. 162, 2023.
- J. Geng, Q. Wen, Q. Zhang, and G. Li, "GNSS observable-specific phase biases
  for all-frequency PPP ambiguity resolution," *Journal of Geodesy*, vol. 96,
  no. 11, pp. 1-20, 2022.
- E. A. Wan and R. van der Merwe, "The unscented Kalman filter for nonlinear
  estimation," *Proceedings of the IEEE Adaptive Systems for Signal Processing,
  Communications, and Control Symposium (AS-SPCC)*, pp. 153-158, 2000.
